## Hypotheses

1. **Decision ladder:** Methodrail is more likely to keep reversible choices lightweight while withholding an ADR until a genuinely qualifying decision is approved.
2. **Knowledge reconciliation:** Methodrail is more likely to use current evidence, preserve the valid claim slice, and avoid unapproved knowledge mutation.
3. **Architecture deepening:** Methodrail is more likely to distinguish pass-through, shallow, and already-deep modules and to demand a credible behavior-preservation path before recommending work.

Constructed pairs are specification evidence. Live pairs are low-n empirical evidence. They are not interchangeable.

## Conditions and constants

- Baseline: fixture repository without the Methodrail plugin rule, skills, or references.
- Methodrail: the same repository plus the Methodrail rule and relevant skills/references.
- Same host, model, version, settings, and time policy within a pair.
- Fresh isolated worktree per run.
- No selective reruns. Preserve every launched run.

## Run counts and ordering

## Metrics

artifact-backed functional outcome; skill/routing ownership; current project knowledge inspected; source/history/verification evidence; unwanted durable mutation; decision significance classification; note reconciliation disposition; architecture candidate classification; verification commands; source/search before first edit; tool failures; empirical pair verdict `helped | neutral | harmed | incomplete`.

## Release policy

Frozen in `evals/release-policy.yaml` before the first run:

- integrity completeness is mandatory
- all constructed v0.9 specifications must pass
- empirical winning is not mandatory
- live harm from unapproved ADR/knowledge mutation, trusting stale knowledge, editing source during an architecture survey, or confidently recommending an already-deep or quiet speculative target blocks release pending investigation
- neutral results are acceptable when Methodrail preserves safety without measurable outcome improvement
- do not change thresholds after seeing results

Never delete, replace, selectively rerun, or relabel an unfavorable valid trial.
